# Brightpath Technologies — Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Brightpath Technologies. It was last updated on 1 April 2026.

## Paid leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid leave per calendar year. Paid leave is credited in equal monthly instalments of 1.5 days.

Employees who join partway through the year receive paid leave on a pro-rata basis from their joining month.

Unused paid leave does not carry over to the next year. Up to 6 unused days can instead be encashed at the end of December, paid at the employee's basic daily salary.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

Sick leave cannot be encashed and lapses at the end of the year.

## Parental leave
Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers and adoptive parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published by HR every December for the following year.

## How to apply
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance, except for sick leave. Requests are approved by the employee's reporting manager.
