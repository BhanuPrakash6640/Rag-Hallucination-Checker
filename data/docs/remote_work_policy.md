# Brightpath Technologies — Remote Work Policy

Brightpath follows a hybrid work model. Employees work from the office 3 days a week, and may work remotely on the remaining 2 working days.

Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory office days for all teams. Each team chooses its third office day.

## Eligibility
Employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing their 3-month probation period. Interns are expected to work from the office 5 days a week.

## Fully remote work
Fully remote work is allowed only with written approval from a department head, for a maximum of 30 days per year.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office allowance of Rs. 15,000 to each eligible employee. The allowance can be used for a chair, desk, monitor or headset. Claims must be submitted with receipts within 60 days of purchase.

The company does not reimburse home internet bills.

## Security
Employees working remotely must connect through the company VPN. Using public Wi-Fi without the VPN is not permitted.
