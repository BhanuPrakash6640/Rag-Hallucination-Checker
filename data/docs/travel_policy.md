# Brightpath Technologies — Travel and Expense Policy

All business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before booking.

## Flights and trains
Domestic flights are booked in economy class. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy with approval from a department head. Train travel is booked in AC 2-tier.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is Rs. 6,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata) and Rs. 4,000 per night in other cities.

## Daily allowance
Employees receive a daily allowance of Rs. 1,200 for meals and local travel during domestic trips. For international trips, the daily allowance is USD 60.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from the trip, with original receipts. Claims are reimbursed in the next salary cycle after finance approval.

Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are not reimbursed.
